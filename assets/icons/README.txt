Put your own icons here:

logo.png      - titlebar logo (20x20 or larger)
folder.png    - yellow tile (game folder)
mods.svg      - red tile (modpacks / mods)
play.png      - green tile (launch, the Enter key)
stop.png      - red tile that replaces play while a game starts (the Esc key)
settings.png  - grey tile (settings)

The white tile needs no icon: the player's own character is drawn there.
